Brewery Tycoon Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2, 2026

Brewery Tycoon does not require users to create an account.

Brewery Tycoon does not directly collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or mailing addresses.

Brewery Tycoon may include optional rewarded advertisements. If ads are added, third-party advertising providers may collect certain data needed to show ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and improve ad delivery. This may include device identifiers, advertising identifiers, usage data, diagnostics, or approximate location, depending on the ad provider and the user's device settings.

Brewery Tycoon may offer a one-time in-app purchase to remove ads. In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Brewery Tycoon does not directly receive or store payment card information.

Users may control certain advertising and privacy settings through their device settings and Apple's privacy controls.

If future versions of Brewery Tycoon add analytics, accounts, cloud saves, leaderboards, or other features that collect additional data, this privacy policy will be updated before those features are released.
