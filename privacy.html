Brewery Tycoon Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Brewery Tycoon

Effective date: August 2, 2026

Information We Collect

Brewery Tycoon does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or mailing addresses.

Advertising

Brewery Tycoon may offer optional rewarded advertisements. When advertising features are included in the game, third-party advertising providers may collect information needed to deliver advertisements, measure performance, prevent fraud, and operate their services.

Depending on the advertising provider and the user's device settings, this information may include device identifiers, advertising identifiers, app usage information, diagnostics, and approximate location.

In-App Purchases

Effective Date: August 2, 2026

Brewery Tycoon may offer a one-time in-app purchase to remove advertisements. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Brewery Tycoon does not directly receive or store payment card information.

Brewery Tycoon does not require users to create an account.

Third-Party Services

Brewery Tycoon does not directly collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or mailing addresses.

Third-party services used by Brewery Tycoon are governed by their own privacy policies. This policy will be updated when advertising, analytics, or other third-party services are added or changed.

Brewery Tycoon may include optional rewarded advertisements. If ads are added, third-party advertising providers may collect certain data needed to show ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and improve ad delivery. This may include device identifiers, advertising identifiers, usage data, diagnostics, or approximate location, depending on the ad provider and the user's device settings.

Children's Privacy

Brewery Tycoon may offer a one-time in-app purchase to remove ads. In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Brewery Tycoon does not directly receive or store payment card information.

Brewery Tycoon does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Users may control certain advertising and privacy settings through their device settings and Apple's privacy controls.

Changes to This Policy

If future versions of Brewery Tycoon add analytics, accounts, cloud saves, leaderboards, or other features that collect additional data, this privacy policy will be updated before those features are released.

This privacy policy may be updated when the game adds or changes features. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
